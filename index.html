Our story

Real Samoan home cooking, from our family to yours

A & F Island Taste is a family-owned bakery, takeaway and catering business on East Street, Ashburton. We bake the food we grew up with — steamed buns, fa'ausi, keke saiga and fresh donuts — alongside hot island meals like lamb chops and sapasui.

Whether it's lunch on the go, a box of donuts for the office, or catering for a family occasion, everything is made fresh and priced fairly.

  • Family owned and operated
  • Traditional Samoan recipes, baked fresh on site
  • Takeaway, event catering and nationwide postage
