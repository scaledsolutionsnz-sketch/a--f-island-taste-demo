Privacy Policy

A & F Island Taste · 516 East Street, Ashburton · Last updated August 2026

1. Overview

A & F Island Taste respects your privacy and handles personal information in line with the Privacy Act 2020 (New Zealand). This page contains placeholder content for demonstration purposes and should be reviewed and completed before public launch.

2. What we collect

When you place an order or contact us, we may collect your name, phone number, email address and order details. This website does not include any forms — contact happens by phone or email at your choice.

3. How we use your information

We use your details only to prepare and fulfil your order, arrange pick-up, catering or postage, and respond to your enquiries. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing.

4. Third-party services

This website embeds services such as Google Maps and links to social media platforms. Those services may collect usage data under their own privacy policies when you interact with them.

5. Storage and security

We keep order information only as long as needed for business and record-keeping purposes, and take reasonable steps to keep it secure.

6. Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you may request access to, or correction of, personal information we hold about you. Contact us and we will respond promptly.

7. Contact

Privacy questions can be directed to A & F Island Taste, 516 East Street, Ashburton, or by phone on 03 307 1322.
